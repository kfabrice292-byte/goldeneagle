Our History

Renforcer les capacités des entreprises burkinabè.

GOLDEN EAGLE SARL

GOLDEN EAGLE is a Limited Liability Company (SARL) specialized in drilling, manufacturing, supplying explosives and blasting services.

We are a company providing practical training in the field of construction, mining and quarrying, and industrial companies due to our solid experience in a booming sector.

Dans le souci de répondre aux normes et exigences internationales, Golden Eagle accompagne ses clients en leur fournissant des consultations et études en évaluation des risques, forage et dynamitage, et en Santé, Hygiène et Environnement.

Our Human Capital: The General Manager has surrounded himself with a dynamic and professional team. Recruitment is done with high selection criteria. Our strength lies in sharing knowledge and experience at all levels, supported by periodic certified training.

Our Infrastructure: The company has three main sites, including the office and garage in Burkina Faso, and an office in Ivory Coast, offering the necessary comfort and safety to employees.

Vision

Increase the productivity of national companies to improve their performance.

Mission

Add value to companies and human capital in the mining and quarrying, construction and similar activities sectors.
