OUR COMPANY

African Mining Expertise at International Standards

IDENTITY & PURPOSE

Operational Rigor & Excellence As Standard

GOLDEN EAGLE SARL is a Burkinabè corporation specialized in precision drilling, manufacturing, and supply of civil explosives and mining blasting services. Our mission is to sustainably maximize the productivity, fragmentation, and safety of open-pit mining operators and civil works across West Africa.

Field Expertise & Operational Infrastructure

Golden Eagle maintains complete infrastructure in Burkina Faso: Executive Headquarters in Ouagadougou (Ouaga 2000) and its dedicated Heavy Machinery Yard and Workshop.

Our fleet features specialized IVECO chemical transport trucks under escort, Toyota Hino utility vehicles, surface drill rigs, and our Mobile Manufacturing Unit (MMU) for on-bench explosive formulation and direct borehole loading.

Leadership & Human Capital

Headed by our Managing Director, a veteran specialist in drilling, mining blasting, and pit risk evaluation, the leadership coordinates an accomplished technical and administrative team: HR management, operational dispatch, finance, master blasters, drillers, Trimble land surveyors, certified welders, and heavy diesel mechanics.

All personnel participate in certified periodic training programs to stay ahead of international mining standards.
