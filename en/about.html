BACKGROUND & HUMAN CAPITAL

GOLDEN EAGLE SARL

GOLDEN EAGLE is a Limited Liability Company (SARL) specialized in drilling, manufacturing, supply of commercial explosives, and high-precision blasting engineering.

We empower extractive industries, quarries, and civil engineering companies by delivering robust field expertise and certified on-site training compliant with international safety benchmarks.

Striving to meet strict health, safety, and environmental (HSE) standards, Golden Eagle provides comprehensive risk assessments, blast design optimization, and fragmentation monitoring.

Our Human Capital: General Management leads a seasoned team of certified blasters, mining engineers, and technicians selected through demanding criteria and continuously trained in modern energetic technologies.

Regional Infrastructure: Golden Eagle operates well-equipped regional bases, technical garages, and field units in Burkina Faso (Ouagadougou & active mining hubs) as well as Côte d'Ivoire.

Strategic Vision

Sustainably enhance the operational efficiency, safety, and profitability of mining operators in West Africa.

Operational Mission

Generate decisive added value for partners through optimized rock fragmentation and secure explosive supply chains.
