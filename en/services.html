Our Services & Solutions

End-to-end industrial capabilities: precision drilling, bench blasting, commercial explosives manufacturing and supply, 3D laser profiling, and certified training.

CORE CAPABILITY

Drilling & Blasting

GOLDEN EAGLE deploys highly seasoned shotfirers and modern drilling fleets. We formulate computer-assisted drill and blast patterns engineered for maximum rock fragmentation while maintaining total vibration and flyrock control.

Technical edge: Rock mass characterization, optimized blast hole spacing, non-electric and electronic initiation, stringent bench safety protocols.

ENERGETIC MATERIALS

Explosives Supply & Manufacturing

We manufacture, transport, and supply certified civil explosives conforming to international HSE standards and mine operational requirements across West Africa.

Products supplied: Matrix Emulsion S100, Porous Prilled Ammonium Nitrate (PPAN), Cast Pentolite Boosters, Instant Electric Detonators (IED), Detonating cords, surface connectors.

AUDITING & CONSULTING

Consultation & Technical Audits

Our senior engineers conduct comprehensive post-blast fragmentation and granulometry analyses on partner sites, significantly reducing downstream crushing and haulage costs.

Scope: Seismograph vibration monitoring, charge-per-delay calibration, powder factor optimization, and boulder reduction strategies.

SAFETY & HSE

Professional Risk Assessment

GOLDEN EAGLE implements rigorous hazard evaluation methodologies covering pyrotechnic handling, operational feasibility, and complete site safety reassurance.

Key areas: Blast evacuation planning, temporary magazine safety, strict compliance with national mining and environmental regulations.

PRECISION GEOMATICS

3D Topography & Drone Surveys

Equipped with high-precision total stations, RTK GPS receivers, and photogrammetry drones, our surveyors digitally map bench faces to eliminate flyrock and reconcile blasted tonnage.

Capabilities: 3D laser face profiling, volume and cubature computation, geological reconciliation, and high-resolution contour mapping.
