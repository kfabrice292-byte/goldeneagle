OUR SERVICES

Integrated Drilling, Mining Blasting & Pyrotechnic Solutions

END-TO-END INDUSTRIAL SOLUTIONS

Technical & Operational Excellence

Golden Eagle deploys certified mining engineers, drillers, and master blasters equipped with modern field assets to maximize blast fragmentation, optimize drilling geometry, and secure mining and quarry operations across West Africa.

Service #1

High-Precision Drilling & Blasting

Deployment of surface drill rigs operated by experienced technicians. Computer-modeled blast design guarantees optimal rock fragmentation while strictly controlling ground vibrations, airblast, and flyrock.

Request a Blast Study
Service #2

Mobile Manufacturing Units (MMU)

Our heavy-duty IVECO chassis-mounted MMU unit provides on-site explosive emulsion formulation and automated borehole pumping, speeding turnaround and securing bulk loading operations in open pits.

Inquire About MMU Service
Service #3

Civil Explosives Supply & Formulation

Supply and manufacture compliant with international quality standards: Porous Prilled Ammonium Nitrate (PPAN), Bulk Emulsion S100, Anfex, Pentolite Boosters, Magnum Boosters, high-security electronic and electric detonators, and Trunline cords.

View Products Catalog
Service #4

Trimble High-Precision Surveying & Volumetrics

Deployment of latest-generation Trimble RTK instruments and aerial surveying drones for precise blast hole layout, 3D bench face photogrammetry, and certified post-blast volumetric calculation.

Request a Surveying Quote
Service #5

Technical Consulting & Pit Risk Assessment

Periodic blast performance audits to refine fragmentation ratios, reduce explosive consumption per cubic meter, and systematically evaluate all environmental and geotechnical risks on site.

Schedule a Technical Audit
Service #6

Certified Professional Blaster Training

Comprehensive classroom and practical training for drill operators, master blasters, assistants, and HSE monitors. Empowering local talent with modern electronic firing systems and open-pit safety best practices.

Explore Training Programs
Service #7

Secure Transport & Armed Convoy Escort

Reliable overland transport of energetic materials utilizing specialized IVECO trucks and Toyota Hino vans under authorized Police and Gendarmerie armed escorts, adhering strictly to regional security regulations.

Plan a Supply Shipment
