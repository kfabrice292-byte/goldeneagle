THE GOLDEN EAGLE TECHNOLOGICAL BENCHMARK

Mobile MMU Unit (Mobile Manufacturing Unit)

A veritable mobile manufacturing plant mounted on an all-terrain carrier, our MMU unit revolutionizes mining operations in Burkina Faso. It carries non-explosive precursors in separate compartments and formulates high-stability emulsion to exact gram specifications directly during borehole charging.

100% Inert Transport

No finished explosive products on public roads. Total safety and complete compliance during highway transit.

Ultra-Fast Delivery

Continuous pumping up to 300 kg/minute, drastically shortening bench downtime and blasting turnaround.

Custom In-Situ Formulation

Density dynamically dialed from 1.05 to 1.25 g/cm³ based on borehole depth and rock hardness.

Full Water Resistance

Bulk emulsion completely displaces water in flooded boreholes without loss of velocity or energy.
